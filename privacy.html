Privacy Policy

Last updated October 5, 2026.

This policy explains how Liscer Inc ("we", "us") collects and uses information from homeowners we contact about residential solar.

Information we collect

Your name, phone number, email, home address, and information you share with us about your home and electric usage, such as a photo of your utility bill. We receive this when you book a solar appointment, when you contact us, or when you reply to our messages.

How we use it

  • To follow up on your solar appointment and answer your questions.
  • To review your electric usage and prepare a solar estimate.
  • To schedule and confirm a home assessment.
  • To share your project details with OC Solar, the installer we sell for, when you ask for a proposal or assessment.

Text messaging

If you receive text messages from us, message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. You can also call or text (818) 814-9908 or email liscerofficial@gmail.com.

We do not sell, rent or share your phone number, and text message opt-in data and consent are never shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

Sharing

We share information only with OC Solar as needed to provide the proposal or service you asked for, with service providers that help us operate (such as our phone and messaging provider) under confidentiality obligations, or when required by law. This sharing never includes text messaging opt-in data or consent.

Your choices

You can ask us to stop contacting you, or to see, correct or delete your information, by emailing liscerofficial@gmail.com or replying STOP to any text.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information.
